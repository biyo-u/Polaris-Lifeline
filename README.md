# Polaris Lifeline

Polaris Lifeline is an ice-jam flood early warning system using data from RCM satellite radar. This demo focused on the town of Hay River, Northwest Territories during a 2022 flood that forced over 3500 people from their homes, and left over 93 million dollars in property damage

We used supervised learning to give an agent examples of flooding (specifically from the Hay River flood in 2022) to compare to examples of normal water levels, and highlighting differences in these images. This enabled us to design a easy-to-use interface that can be easily integrated into emergency response protocols.

Designed by Henry Uvbiama, Daniela Raz, and Munir Al-Taher for the 2026 Mission Accepted Space Hackathon.
